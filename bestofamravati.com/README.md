# Best of Amravati (BOA) - Civic Business Directory

A modern Next.js 14 web application matching the **Civic Crimson Directory** design system for Amravati, Maharashtra.

## UI Pages Included

1. **Home Portal (`/`)**
   - Trademarked BOA logo hero badge with radial glow backdrop.
   - Radiant gradient headline: *"Search Top 5 Best of Amravati"*.
   - Interactive category search omnibox with instant filter pills (Restaurants, Sarafa Bazar, IT & Repairs, Clinics).
   - 10 Featured Category Cards (Food, Fashion, Jewelry, Health, Education, Home, Hotels, Services, Events, Auto).
   - Civic assurance banner & verification badges.
   - Official reference showcase.
   - Interactive floating **🚀 Let's Chat** Civic Helpdesk widget.

2. **Top 5 Software & IT Companies (`/services`)**
   - Breadcrumbs navigation.
   - Civic trust certified banner & peer review stats (100% Locally Verified, 157+ Reviews).
   - Filter & sort bar (Ranked by Rating, Verified Only, Open Now, Amravati zones).
   - 5 Ranked company listings:
     - **01: BIZONANCE INDIA PVT. LTD.** (Featured Champion, 5.0 ★, 47 reviews, custom software tags, address, call/direction actions, link to profile)
     - **02: TechNova Solutions Amravati** (4.9 ★, 38 reviews, Camp Road)
     - **03: Apex Digital & Softwares** (4.8 ★, 29 reviews, Badnera Road)
     - **04: CodeCraft Studios** (4.8 ★, 24 reviews, Rajapeth)
     - **05: Infowave Systems** (4.7 ★, 19 reviews, Gadge Nagar)
   - Civic evaluation criteria banner & listing claim link.

3. **Bizonance India Pvt. Ltd. Profile (`/business/bizonance` and `/business`)**
   - Cover image banner with "12 Photos" & "BOA Verified Business".
   - Overlapping business identity card with "B INDIA" monogram and verification badge.
   - Operating hours and status: *"Open · Closes 7 pm"*.
   - Tagline & company description banner.
   - Social media channels (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, X).
   - Interactive tabs: **Services**, **Products**, **Photos**, **Videos**.
   - Expandable service categories (Software Company with 11 specialized services, Marketing Agency, Advertising Agency).
   - 5.0 Star rating distribution graph (100% 5-star ratings).
   - 10 Verified client reviews with avatars and timestamps.
   - Right sidebar: About Us description, Key Highlights grid (2020, Amravati, 5.0/5.0, MCA/ISO), Follow Us card, Office Location map card with Directions link, and original archival catalog snapshot.

4. **Supporting Pages**
   - **About Us (`/about-us`)**: Mission, verification policy, and ranking criteria.
   - **Contact (`/contact`)**: Bureau address, contact numbers, and interactive message form.
   - **Join Us (`/join-us`)**: Business claim and onboarding verification audit submission form.

## Tech Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js 14 (App Router)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with custom Civic Crimson color palette (`#b7131a`)
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter
- **Icons:** Material Symbols Outlined
- **Language:** TypeScript

## Getting Started

To run the development server:
```bash
pnpm dev
# or
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) with your browser to view the application.
